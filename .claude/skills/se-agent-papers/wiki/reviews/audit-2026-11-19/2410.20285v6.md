## Sections and Appendices

- Sections 1–3. The agent works within a repository. A modified Monte Carlo tree search uses state values and textual explanations to choose branches. A separate mechanism compares the final candidate solutions. The study also evaluates Moatless-Adapted, which relaxes previously rigid state transitions. ## Independent check

The percentage-improvement column in Table 1 divides the difference by the **final score**. For GPT-4o, (31.0 − 25.7)/31.0 = 17.1%, while the conventional relative increase over baseline is 5.3/25.7 = **20,6%**. Across five models, the mean of the published absolute differences is **4.96 percentage points**, and the mean relative increase over the respective baselines was approximately **29,5%**. The authors obtain 23% using a different normalization. Table 3 gives an increase in GPT-4o cost from USD 40.86 to USD 576.
