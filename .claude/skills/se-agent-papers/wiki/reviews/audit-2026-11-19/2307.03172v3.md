# Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts

Review of version `2307.03172v3` • 19 November 2026 • 18 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172v3) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2307.03172.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2307.03172.md)

## Section review

- **1–2. Multi-document questions.** Across 2,655 NaturalQuestions-Open examples, the authors vary the number of Wikipedia distractors and the position of the answer-bearing document. Contriever selects distractors, excluding documents that contain known answers. A response counts as correct if its generated text contains any reference answer. Figures 1–5 show the setup, inputs and position effect. Table 1 includes baselines with no documents and with only the answer-bearing document. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=5)
- **3–4. Key lookup and analysis of the position effect.** The synthetic task uses random identifiers and varies the dictionary size. Repeating the question before and after the data nearly eliminates key-lookup errors, but QA accuracy still varies by position. Encoder–decoder models are more robust at sequence lengths seen in training. Both the base and instruction-tuned MPT-30B exhibit a position effect. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=9)
- **5–7. Retrieval, related work and conclusions.** In open-domain retrieval, answer accuracy levels off before retrieval recall. Increasing the number of documents from 20 to 50 yields only a small gain for GPT-3.5-Turbo and Claude-1.3. The authors relate their results to earlier long-context studies and propose measuring the gap between the best and worst document positions. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=3) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=9) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=10) [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=11)
- **References and Appendices A–C.** Separate controls used unambiguous questions, randomly sampled distractors in place of semantically similar ones, and randomized document order disclosed in the prompt. The accuracy dip persisted as the overall difficulty changed (Figures 12–14). [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=11) [PDF, p. 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=12) [PDF, p. 13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=13) [PDF, p. 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=14) [PDF, p. 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=15)
- **D–E.** GPT-4 was evaluated on 500 randomly selected questions with 20 documents each. For Llama-2, the authors excluded 20 of 2,655 examples that exceeded the context window. The 7B model showed its strongest advantage at the end, while the 13B and 70B models performed better at both ends. [PDF, p. 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=15) [PDF, p. 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=16)
- **F–G, Tables 2–7.** Token counts vary by tokenizer. For GPT-3.5-Turbo with 20 documents, the mean was 2,946.2 tokens and the maximum was 3,920. The last three tables report accuracy at each document position with 10, 20 and 30 documents. Position indices start at zero. [PDF, p. 17](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=17) [PDF, p. 18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=18)

## Independent verification

In Table 6, with 20 documents, GPT-3.5-Turbo scored 75.8% at index 0, 53.8% at index 9, and 63.2% at index 19. The difference between the first and tenth positions is **22.0 percentage points** The score at the middle position is **2.3 percentage points below** 56.1% for the no-document baseline in Table 1. The tabulated values independently confirm the earlier reading of the curve. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=5) [PDF, p. 18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.03172v3#page=18)
