# ColBERTv2: Effective and Efficient Retrieval via Lightweight Late Interaction

Review of version `2112.01488v3` • 19 November 2026 • 20 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01488v3) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2112.01488.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2112.01488.md)

## Section review

- **1–3.** For each query token, ColBERTv2 still selects the most similar token representation in a document and sums these similarities. It adds training with hard negatives and soft scores from a cross-encoder. At indexing time it stores the nearest centroid and the residual, quantized to one or two bits per dimension. Figure 1 shows where query–document interaction occurs. - **5–6, Tables 4–5.** On the official MS MARCO development split, the model achieved 39.7 MRR@10 and 98.4 Recall@1000, compared with 36.0 and 96.8 for the earlier ColBERT. A separate local dataset contains 5,000 questions. BEIR results vary by dataset. On HotpotQA, SPLADEv2 scores 68.4 and ColBERTv2 scores 66.7. On the pooled LoTTE dataset, ColBERTv2 achieves Success@5 of 71.6 on search and 63.4 on forum questions. - **Limitations, references and Appendices A–C.** Evaluation uses English, and out-of-domain tests cover models trained on MS MARCO. The cluster analysis compares token distributions and gives examples (Figure 2 and Table 6). Compressing the original ColBERT alone changes MRR@10 from 36.2 to 36.2 with 2 bits and to 35.5 with 1 bit. Figure 3 compares time and quality across retrieval settings and datasets. The reported latencies of about 50–250 ms apply to this configuration. ## Independent verification

Size **entire index** for MS MARCO fell from 154 GiB to 16 GiB or 25 GiB, giving respective compression ratios of **9,625×** and **6,16×**. The index size includes 4.5 GiB of inverted lists. More aggressive compression yields the higher ratio. The 39.7 MRR@10 result concerns the main model with modified training, while 36.2 and 35.5 come from a separate compression ablation on the older model.
