# AgenticFlict: A Large-Scale Dataset of Merge Conflicts in AI Coding Agent Pull Requests on GitHub

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2604.03551v2`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2604.03551.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03551v2) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2604.03551.metadata.json)

## Findings and evidence

- **1. Problem and 2. Dataset construction.** From 932,791 pull requests in AIDev, the authors selected 142,652 that were open or had been closed without merging. Figure 1 and Algorithm 1 describe how merges were reconstructed. The authors used current identifiers for open requests and attempted to recover the base revision at closure for closed ones. The identifier of the last change to a file serves as a proxy for the origin of a conflict. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=3)
- **3.1–3.2. Design.** Figure 2 distinguishes the repository, PR, file, region, and commit that last modified the region. A PR can contain multiple files and regions. Table 1 reports an average of 4.36 files and 11.36 regions per conflicting PR, with 336,380 regions in total. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=5)
- **4. Exploratory analysis.** Figure 3 and Table 2 compare agents. Figure 4 shows a strongly skewed distribution of line counts. In Figure 5, the conflict rate rises through the middle deciles of change size and falls for the largest changes. These observations come from a selected sample and cannot establish whether agent brand or pull request size causes conflicts. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=6)
- **5–7. Related work, validity, and conclusions.** The authors compare their labels with studies of PR acceptance and merge benchmarks. The method omits semantic conflicts, conflicts resolved before merging, and histories that cannot be reconstructed. The authors propose a human comparison group drawn from the same repositories. In the main PDF, the acknowledgments and references on pages 7–9 are the final sections. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=9)
- **Separate supplement, Appendices A–E (8 pages).** Appendix A documents RAW and CLEAN, the relationships, field tables, and mapping. Appendix B specifies how to reproduce one label, C illustrates grouping by agent, D describes applications, and E lists limitations, including the changing status of open PRs. The schema on page 2 should be interpreted alongside the actual status values in CLEAN. ## Discrepancy reproduced from the released data

The reviewer independently grouped all 142,652 rows of `data/clean/agenticflict_pr_clean.csv` according to `status_code` and `conflict_label` in Zenodo record 20118379. There are 29,609 TRUE and 75,924 FALSE values, making 105,533 classified labels. Another 1,493 rows have `ERR_MERGE_FAILED` with no label. The other 35,626 records comprise 29,598 rows without a status, 4,683 with a historical base resolution error, and 1,345 with an API error. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.03551v2#page=5) [Replication package version](https://zenodo.org/records/20118379)

Published **27,67%** is 29,609/107,026, where 107,026 comprises 105,533 classified PRs and 1,493 unresolved PRs. **28,06%** is 29,609/105,533 among pull requests with a known conflict or no-conflict label. For Copilot and Codex, Table 2 gives 15.24% and 31.85%, respectively, with unresolved attempts included in the denominators. Page 5 gives 15.43% and 32.31%, consistent with the labeled subsets (2,583/16,736 and 23,520/72,789). The card gives both overall estimates and their denominators.
