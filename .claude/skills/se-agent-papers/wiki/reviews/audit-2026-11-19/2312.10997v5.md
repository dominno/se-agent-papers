# Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey

Review of version `2312.10997v5` • 19 November 2026 • 21 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997v5) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2312.10997.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2312.10997.md)

## Section review

- **I–II, Figures 1–4.** The survey classifies systems by when they incorporate retrieval, during pretraining, fine-tuning or inference. A separate distinction between basic, advanced and modular RAG describes the workflow. Basic RAG indexes the data, retrieves passages and generates an answer from the context. Advanced RAG improves processing before and after retrieval. Modular RAG allows stages to be replaced or reordered. ## Independent verification

The reviewer checked Table IV against the original RAGAS paper. The survey lists context relevance, faithfulness and answer relevance. RAGAS evaluates each dimension separately through pairwise comparisons. The naive, advanced and modular RAG taxonomy is the survey authors’ synthesis. Tables I–IV contain no common experiment that could establish the advantage of one architecture over another.
