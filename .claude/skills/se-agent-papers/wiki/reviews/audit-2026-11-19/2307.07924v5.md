# ChatDev: Communicative Agents for Software Development

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2307.07924v5`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2307.07924.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924v5) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2307.07924.metadata.json)

## Findings and evidence

- **1–2. Objective and related work.** Figure 1 illustrates communication across stages of software development. The authors discuss earlier models specialized for individual stages and agents equipped with memory and tools. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=3)
- **3.1-3.2. Architecture.** Figure 2 divides the process into design, coding, code completion, review and testing. Two agents with different roles converse within each subtask. The current stage retains the full dialogue, while subsequent stages receive its output. The assistant can ask for clarification before changing the program. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=5)
- **4. Data and protocol.** SRDD contains 1,200 prompts across 40 subcategories. The comparison with GPT-Engineer and MetaGPT uses ChatGPT-3.5, temperature 0.2 and Python 3.11.4 throughout. The metrics assess whether code is free of placeholders, whether it runs, and how well its semantic representation matches the requirements. Quality is the product of these component scores. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=6)
- **4.1. Result and cost.** Table 1 reports executability of 0.8800 for ChatDev, 0.4145 for MetaGPT and 0.3583 for GPT-Engineer. Across both comparisons in Table 2, human judges preferred ChatDev more often than the GPT-4 judge did. In Table 3, ChatDev averaged 148.21 seconds and about 22,949 tokens, compared with 15.60 seconds and about 7,183 tokens for GPT-Engineer. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=7)
- **4.2. Ablations.** In Table 4, removing the roles lowers Quality from 0.3953 to 0.2212, and removing the clarification stage lowers it to 0.3094. Completeness peaks at 0.6250 after code completion and ends at 0.5600. Individual components can therefore decline even as the composite score improves. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=7)
- **4.3, 5–6. Communication and limitations.** Figure 3 shows the distribution of utterances, Figure 4 traces the reviews, and Figure 5 tracks errors across successive runs. The authors discuss prototyping and limitations such as substituting static values for a database. The metrics do not establish full functionality or usability. The references follow the acknowledgments. There are no appendices. ## Independent verification of the paper record

Recalculation from Table 1 gives 0.5600 × 0.8800 × 0.8021 = 0.39527488, which rounds to the reported 0.3953. The difference in the proportion of runnable outputs relative to MetaGPT is 46.55 percentage points. The paper card now identifies the measured quantity and baseline in place of a general interpretation of the role of languages.
