## Text, tables, and plots

Sections 1–2 and Figure 1. TRACE studies how model answers vary across stages of a visual task. Section 3 distinguishes writing questions directly from writing them from an earlier solution. Dependencies form a directed acyclic graph. FFS identifies the first step at which an ultimately incorrect trajectory diverges from the majority answer. Detecting that divergence does not establish that it caused the error. ## Check of examples and appendix

Table 2 and its commentary say that angle A is half of AOC. Inspection of the rendered PDF confirms that the central angle labeled correct is 80°. The stated relation would give 40°, whereas the final answer is 25°. This contradiction makes the example unsuitable as evidence of a correct diagnosis. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.05943v3#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.05943v3#page=9)

Examples II–V (Tables 3–6) cover a chord, the foci of an ellipse, area ratios and reflection of a point. The chord has length 2√(13² − 5²) = 24. In the reflection example, a segment joins (3, 1) and (2, 2). Reflecting (3, 2) across x + y = 4 gives (2, 1). Both solution paths fail in the ellipse example. Appendices A.1–A.2 and Tables 7–8 compare exploration with reuse of an existing solution path and give a magnet example. A.3–A.5, Tables 9–12 and Figures 6–8 cover temperature, filtering, question count and the full generation prompt. Before filtering, GPT-4.1 drops by 6.1 percentage points on MMMUPro and Maverick by 9.0 points on TIGER. Mean question counts during exploration are 6.13 and 6.29, respectively, compared with 4.13 and 3.70 when reusing a previous path. The prose approximation of six versus four obscures the effect of strategy.
