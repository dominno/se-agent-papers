## Audit procedure

- Sections 1–3. The authors distinguish atomic file renaming from data durability after a crash. Prior work on file systems and distributed training motivates testing them separately. The experiments used one APFS file system and small synthetic tensors. A process interruption served as the crash. ## Independent check

The p99 times for directory synchronization and writing without synchronization were 20.27 ms and 3.02 ms, respectively. Their rounded ratio is **6,71**, and the increase was approximately **571%**. The discussion’s “5.7×” wording conflates a time ratio with the relative increase. Range zeroing also gives 399/400 = 99.75%. Table 2 reports an upper interval bound of 30.8% for 0/10. The two-sided Wilson interval from Appendix B, with z = 1.96, gives 3.8416/(10 + 3.8416) = **27,75%**. The interruption experiments included no group directly testing the claimed resilience of the atomic modes.
