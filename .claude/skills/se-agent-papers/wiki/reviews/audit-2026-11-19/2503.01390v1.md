## Section review

- Sections 1–2, Figures 1–2, and Listings 1–2. Correlations among application updates narrow the set of crash states worth testing. POSIX and memory-mapped examples illustrate how representative states are chosen and how heuristic assumptions can reduce coverage. - §§5–6, Tables 1–3, Figures 9–10 and Listings 3–4. The eight production variants had 18 bugs: 2 atomicity bugs, 9 ordering bugs, 5 unpersisted-write bugs and 2 recovery bugs. Seven were new. The separate MMIO microbenchmarks yielded 102 bugs, including 49 new ones. The comparisons with ALICE and Jaaru yielded factors of 4× and 8×, respectively, within a two-hour budget on different populations. For POSIX RocksDB, Table 3 gives 37,478 states checked versus 16,477,850 in budget-limited exhaustive search. Code coverage and the causes of two failures were separate observations.
