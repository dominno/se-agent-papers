# Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models

## Review of sections and appendices

- Sections 1–2 and Figures 1–5. An external model sets the goal for the next stage of Minecraft exploration. A library stores executable skills that the agent can retrieve and compose. Environmental feedback, execution errors, and an LLM-based verifier guide iterative revision of the program. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16291v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16291v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16291v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16291v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.16291v2#page=5)
- Section 3, Figures 6–10 and Tables 1–2. At a limit of 160 iterations, Voyager discovered 63 distinct items, about 3.3 times the baseline count. It reached the iron tier in 3/3 trials after 21 ± 7 iterations on average, and diamond in 1/3 after 102 iterations in that successful trial. In a new world, it completed all four tasks in 3/3 trials under a 50-iteration limit. Without the library it still succeeded 3/3 times on three tasks and 2/3 on the diamond pickaxe, usually more slowly.
