Section 4, Tables 1–2 and Figures 5–7. A single edit by Kimi-K3 yields gains of 0.616 − 0.529 = 0.087, 0.170 − 0.029 = 0.141 and 0.374 − 0.323 = 0.051. The one-line instruction control yields smaller gains. Five rounds of self-editing also incorporate execution traces and verifier feedback. For verification errors, the difference increases from 0.003 after a single edit to 0.104 with self-editing.
