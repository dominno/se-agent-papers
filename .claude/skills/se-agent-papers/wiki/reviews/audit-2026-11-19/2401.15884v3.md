# Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation

Independent audit on 19 November 2026 • 2401.15884v3 · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2401.15884.md)

## Section-by-section review

- **1–3, Figure 1.** The method corrects cases where a retriever returns evidence unrelated to the question. The authors compare it with methods that decide whether to retrieve at all and systems that retrieve context during generation. Figure 1 shows how a mismatched document can lead a generator to identify the wrong person. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=3)
- **4, Figure 2 and Algorithm 1.** T5-large (0.77 billion parameters) scores question–document pairs. If the highest score exceeds the upper threshold, the system selects passages from the corpus. If every score falls below the lower threshold, it uses a web search engine. Between the thresholds, it combines both sources. Search results are split into passages, filtered and passed to the generator. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=6)
- **5.1–5.3, Table 1.** The comparison uses the Contriever result from Self-RAG. PopQA, PubHealth and ARC use accuracy, while Biography uses FactScore. CRAG outperforms standard RAG in all eight combinations of four tasks and two generators. Self-CRAG improves on Self-RAG in six comparisons and scores slightly lower in two. The table distinguishes results taken from earlier work from the configuration reproduced by the authors. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=4) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=8)
- **5.4–5.8, Tables 2–6 and Figure 3.** On PopQA, removing any of the three actions lowers performance, as does disabling passage filtering, query rewriting or source selection. Document relevance assessment accuracy is 84.3% for T5 and 58.0–64.7% across three ChatGPT prompting methods. Artificially removing relevant results hurts both methods, with a smaller decline for Self-CRAG in Figure 3. Table 5 isolates the effect of adding web sources. The Table 6 timings of 0.363 seconds for RAG and 0.512 seconds for CRAG cover generation only and exclude retrieval and data processing. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=9) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=10)
- **6, conclusions and limitations.** The method requires fine-tuning an external evaluator. End-to-end performance also depends on web sources and the thresholds chosen. The references include Self-RAG, Contriever and FactScore. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=6) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=10) [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=11) [PDF, p. 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=12) [PDF, p. 13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=13)
- **Appendix A, Tables 7–10.** The paper gives a prompt for converting a question into keywords and three variants of the relevance-assessment prompt. Varying the prompt forms part of the empirical comparison with T5. [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=9) [PDF, p. 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=14)
- **Appendix B.1–B.5 and Table 11.** The PopQA test included 1,399 questions about rare entities. The remaining questions were used to tune the evaluator. The model transfers to other tasks, with different threshold settings across datasets. The Table 11 caption describes removing an action. The rows and Appendix B.4 instead describe forcing a single identical action across all examples. Appendix B.5 attributes the untuned generator’s lower PubHealth score in part to answers that did not follow the required format. ## Independent verification

With the SelfRAG-LLaMA2-7b generator, CRAG gains 7.0, 14.9, 36.6 and 15.4 points over standard RAG on the four tasks in Table 1. In the same block, Self-CRAG and Self-RAG score 67.2 and 67.3 on ARC (−0.1 point). With LLaMA2-hf-7b, the corresponding PubHealth scores are 0.6 and 0.7 (−0.1 point). Whether a correction improves the score depends on the baseline. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884v3#page=7)
