## Independent check of the paper card’s findings

The review compared the examples and claims in Figures 2, 3, 6 and 7. The figures confirm the architecture and show how it responds to changing requirements. The claim that the system chooses better moments to ask for clarification comes from the authors' qualitative observation in §2.4. The paper defines no metric, sample size or control group for this result. The paper card now identifies it as qualitative. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07056v1#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07056v1#page=6) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07056v1#page=8)

A second reviewer independently confirmed that the reported improvement in interaction on page 6 is a qualitative observation by the authors. The validator inspects the state snapshot after an operation and can trigger replanning. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07056v1#page=6)
