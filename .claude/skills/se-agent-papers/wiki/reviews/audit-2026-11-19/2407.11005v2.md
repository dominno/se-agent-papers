The model actually trained was **DeBERTa**, although the abstract names RoBERTa. In Table 3, DeBERTa achieves the highest AUROC on 11 of 12 datasets. The exception is DelucionQA (RAGAS 0.70, DeBERTa 0.64). The ExpertQA value of 0.87 and the TAT-QA relevance RMSE of 0.27 exceed the upper bounds in the text (0.86 and 0.26). ## Independent check

In Table 4, example-level agreement with human judgments is **76% before exclusion** the “Neither” category and **93% after exclusion**. Quoting 93% without describing which examples were retained overstates expected agreement on the full dataset. Relevance and utilization were assessed on a separate set of 40 examples. On ExpertQA, the hallucination-detection AUROC gain over the best of the other three judges is 0.87 − 0.70 = 0.17.
