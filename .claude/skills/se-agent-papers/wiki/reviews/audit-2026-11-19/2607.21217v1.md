## Study design and sections

Sections I–II, Figure 1 and Table I contrast building a repository from an incomplete description with repairing existing code. Section III, Figures 2–5 and Tables II–V explain how working programs were used to derive full specifications, cases based on original tests and additional robustness tests. The full version has 480 projects in 12 languages. Lite has 50 projects in 10 languages. ## Results, independent calculations and appendices

Section IV, Figures 6–10 and Tables VI–IX. Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 pass 38.2% and 37.2% of cases on Full, versus 48.2% and 53.3% on Lite. Ranking six models by the Overall columns gives a sum of squared rank differences of 10 and Spearman correlation of 1 − 6 × 10/(6 × 35) = 0.714. Supplying runnable public test files raises GLM-5.1 think-8k from 37.4% to 61.8% (+24.4 percentage points), while coverage of discovered constraints falls from 63.8% to 61.2%. Tables X–XV distinguish the agent harness, client model, question count, reasoning mode, system image and language. For GLM-5.1, raising the budget from 16 to 24 questions improves constraint coverage but lowers the score from 37.4% to 34.4%. The 25.8 and 21.9 values in Table XV give +3.9 for Go, matching the table. Sections V–VI, Table XVI and Figure 11 distinguish correctness from judge opinion, runtime and cost. Across 200 repositories the judge’s correlations with three annotators are 0.372–0.471.
