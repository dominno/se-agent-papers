## Follow-up search and independent proof check, 20 November 2026

Equation (1) can be verified from the definitions on page 3. Let A be the event that the gate accepts. For a deterministic gate, the sum of its two conditional error probabilities is 1 − P₊(A) + P₋(A). The definition of total variation bounds P₊(A) − P₋(A) from above by TV(P₊, P₋). For a randomized gate, condition on its independent random seed and average the same inequality. This establishes the stated lower bound for a decision based on agent-visible evidence.
