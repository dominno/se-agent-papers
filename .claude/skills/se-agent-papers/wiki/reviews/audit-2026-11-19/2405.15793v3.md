## Review of sections and appendices

- §§1–3 and Figures 1–3. The agent–computer interface (ACI) combines search, browsing, editing and environment calls. Syntax-checked editing and a limited file view affect both feedback and the number of decisions required of the model. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15793v3#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15793v3#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15793v3#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15793v3#page=4)
- Sections 4–5, Tables 1–3, and Figures 4–8. GPT-4 Turbo resolved 12.47% of 2,294 SWE-bench issues from 12 Python projects at an average cost of USD 1.59. For RAG with **the same model** the result is 1.31% at USD 0.13, compared with 3.79% at USD 0.25 for Claude 3 Opus. Holding the model fixed, the difference is 11.16 percentage points. Lite contains 300 tasks. Editing without linting yields 15%, adding linting yields 18%, and removing the dedicated editor yields 10.3%. HumanEvalFix evaluates Python, JavaScript, and Java separately.
