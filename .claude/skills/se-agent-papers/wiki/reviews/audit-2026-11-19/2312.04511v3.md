# An LLM Compiler for Parallel Function Calling

Independent audit on 19 November 2026 • 2312.04511v3 · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2312.04511.md)

## Architecture, results and independent calculation

§§1–2 and Figure 1 compare sequential ReAct actions with a plan that separates independent searches. In §§3–4 and Figure 2, a planner specifies a dependency graph. Each action starts as soon as its arguments are available, even while the plan is still being generated. If a dependency emerges during execution, the system can plan a further stage. §5, Figure 3 and Tables 1–2. On Movie Recommendation, the runtimes for GPT (20.47 and 5.47 seconds) and LLaMA-2 (33.37 and 11.83 seconds) imply speedups of 3.74× and 2.82× over ReAct with an additional instruction. For GPT, the reported cost per thousand examples falls from $20.46 to $3.04. On HotpotQA, GPT's 62.00% accuracy is slightly below the 62.47% for ReAct with the additional instruction. ## Appendices

Appendices A–B, Figures A.1–A.4. ReAct sometimes terminates early or repeats calls. The first paragraph of Appendix A assigns 70.00 and 77.80 to HotpotQA, although Table 1 lists them for Movie Recommendation. In ParallelQA, 3 of 36 errors examined were attributed to planning. Appendices C–D and Table C.1 show streaming reduces ParallelQA time from 21.72 to 16.69 seconds. The authors document models, parameters and three repetitions of the GPT accuracy evaluation.
