## Sections, Tables and Appendices

- Sections 1–2 ask how to identify the first critical error in a failed run. The pilot distinguishes transient errors from errors that persist to the end. Of 331 local errors, 205 were subsequently corrected. - Sections 3–4 use three levels of context detail to identify signs of errors, track their subsequent state and attribute the final cause. TrajErrBench contains 400 customer-service and 86 software-engineering runs. Where annotators disagreed in the software-engineering group, the published dataset retained cases with a majority vote.
