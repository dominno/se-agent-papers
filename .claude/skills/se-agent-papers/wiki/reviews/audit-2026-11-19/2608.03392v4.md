## Section review

- §§1–3, Tables 1–2 and Figures 1–4. The review distinguishes solving one task from making an update that remains available on subsequent tasks. The taxonomy records where the system is modified and when it accepts a change. Figure 3 classifies 108 methods. That count gives the number of methods in the survey, which were not evaluated in a shared experiment.
