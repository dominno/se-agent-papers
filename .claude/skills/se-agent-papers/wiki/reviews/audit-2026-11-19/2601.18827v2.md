# Automated structural testing of LLM-based agents: methods, framework, and case studies

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2601.18827v2`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2601.18827.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18827v2) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2601.18827.metadata.json)

## Findings and evidence

- **I–II. Problem and related work.** Inspecting only the final response makes it difficult to check tool selection or locate the cause of an error. Figure 1 separates perception, planning, and execution. The survey of tools and earlier work on automation costs provides background and does not measure the effect of the authors’ method. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=4)
- **III.A–C. Method.** Each turn has its own trace, and a shared identifier groups the turns in a conversation. Assertions check recorded calls, and a model stub forces a specified reply. Table I gives component-test examples and Table II gives three interaction examples. The authors note that replacing every component limits the value of an integration test. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=5)
- **IV.A–C. Use.** The paper covers regression testing, diagnostics, multiple languages, and multiple turns. Figure 2 organizes component, integration, and acceptance tests. A trace can be replayed as a failing test so that developers can correct the behavior before refactoring. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=6)
- **V.A. Driver assistance.** Figure 3 presents the prototype. Listing 1 checks reading and updating data. Listing 2 uses controlled and real model replies to test a booking. The total row in the rendered Figure 4 shows 136 statements, 20 branches, and reported coverage of 96%. It is one report spanning three files. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=7)
- **V.B–C. Incident diagnosis.** Figure 5 and the text illustrate controlling the order in which architecture, logs, and metrics are retrieved and testing documentation changes. The authors report qualitatively that traces make causes easier to find. They provide neither a diagnosis-time measurement nor a control group. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.18827v2#page=8)
- **VI. Conclusions and availability.** The implementation uses the Amazon Bedrock Converse API. The authors released the sample travel agent and its tests. Transfer to other interfaces and quantitative effectiveness remain open. The references occupy pp. 9–10. The main PDF has no appendices.
