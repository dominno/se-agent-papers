§§1–3 and Table 1 describe four simulated environments. Two properties of the final state are checked independently: whether the system completed the user's instruction and whether a security violation occurred. Across the environments, 40 + 21 + 20 + 16 = 97 user tasks and 6 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 27 security goals yield 629 permissible combinations.
