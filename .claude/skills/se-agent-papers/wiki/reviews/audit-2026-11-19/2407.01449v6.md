## Review of sections and appendices

- Sections 1–3. The task is to retrieve the right page image for a text query. Figures 1–2 compare image indexing with multistage extraction of text, tables, and plots. Table 1 describes the ViDoRe evaluation datasets, including English and French documents. - §§4–5. ColPali adapts PaliGemma-3B and matches each query token to the most similar image patch. Table 2 reports nDCG@5 on ten datasets. The mean is 81.3, compared with 58.8 for single-vector BiPali and 67.0 for BGE-M3 with captions. Some text-based methods could not be evaluated on purely visual material. ## Independent verification

Averaging the ten ColPali cells in Table 2 gives 81.25, or 81.3 after rounding. Table 6 reports 72.7 for the corresponding first-hit metric. On DocVQA, Table 4 gives per-page storage of 257.5/8.60 ≈ 29.9 times that of BGE-M3, with ColPali measured in float16. On Shift in Table 2, ColPali and BGE-M3 with OCR both score 73.2. The average advantage therefore includes a tie on an individual dataset.
