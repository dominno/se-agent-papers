# RAGChecker: A Fine-grained Framework for Diagnosing Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2408.08067v2`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2408.08067.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08067v2) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2408.08067.metadata.json)

## Section review

- Sections 1–3 and Figure 1. The system breaks the answer and reference text into claims, then tests whether the answer, reference, or retrieved passages entail them. The joint precision and recall metrics, the retriever’s claim coverage, and the generator’s faithfulness and sensitivity to noise use different denominators. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=5)
- §§4.1–4.2 and Tables 1–2. The main dataset contains 4,162 queries from ten domains. The meta-evaluation compares preferences on 280 pairs. For overall quality, Pearson's r is 0.6193, compared with 0.4831 for RAGAS Answer Similarity and 0.7009 between human assessors. Spearman's correlation for completeness is an exception to the claim of an advantage on every dimension: 0.5811 versus 0.6135 for RAGAS. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=7)
- Sections 4.3–5 and Table 3. The eight systems combine two retrievers with four generators. GPT-4’s mean F1 is 52.7 with E5-Mistral and 50.3 with BM25. Switching retrievers raises claim coverage from 74.0 to 83.5, while GPT-4’s sensitivity to noise within passages judged relevant also rises, from 26.2 to 28.9. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.08067v2#page=9)
- Appendices A–D, Tables 4–5 and Figures 2–5. The authors describe data provenance, licenses, GPT-4 expansion of some short reference answers, corpus reduction, formulas and annotation rules. The complete correlation table expands on the main text, and a prompt example specifies the default generator. - Appendices E–I, Tables 6–16 and Figures 6–10. The domain sheets cover ClapNQ, NovelQA, seven RobustQA domains and KIWI. The analyses vary top-k, chunk size and overlap, and the prompt. In Table 16, validation of the Llama 3 extractor and judge in noisy context gives 71.75% accuracy, leaving room for measurement error. The authors discuss limits on diagnosing retriever errors and possible social effects.
