# Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations

Review of version `2305.14627v2` • 19 November 2026 • 24 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627v2) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2305.14627.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2305.14627.md)

## Sections 1–8

- Setup and data (§§1–2, Figure 1 and Table 1). The system must answer the question and cite specific 100-word corpus passages. ASQA tests ambiguous factual questions, QAMPARI tests lists of answers and ELI5 tests longer explanations. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=3)
- Evaluation and modeling (Sections 3–4, Figures 2–3, Tables 2–3). Agreement with reference answers is measured separately for each task. Citation completeness requires the cited passages collectively to support a claim. Citation precision detects references that are unnecessary for that support. The authors compare supplying sources before answering, retrieving during generation, and adding citations or selecting an answer afterward. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=6)
- Experiments and human evaluation (§§5–6). Tables 4–7 compare strategies and models. Figure 4 distinguishes retriever capability from generator correctness. In Tables 8–9, humans and ALCE rank the three methods in the same order for completeness on both datasets. Matching rankings do not establish agreement on every judgment. Incomplete ELI5 reference answers and errors by the entailment classifier complicate the interpretation of individual scores. - The audit checked Tables 6 and 21. For ChatGPT on ELI5, selecting among four answers raises citation recall from 51.1 to 69.3 (+18.2 points) while coverage of reference claims falls from 12.0 to 11.4. The earlier “about half of cases” requires a configuration and a unit of analysis, namely a statement in the answer. Table 11 gives ChatGPT citation precision of 63.0 on ASQA, whereas the full Table 19 gives 72.5 for the same described variant.
