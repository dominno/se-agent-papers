# AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2308.03688v3`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2308.03688.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688v3) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2308.03688.metadata.json)

## Section review

- §§1–3, Table 1 and Figures 1–2. AgentBench formalizes a multi-turn task with observations and actions. Its eight environments cover an operating system, a database, a knowledge graph, a card game, lateral-thinking puzzles, a household simulation, shopping and web navigation. - Section 4, Tables 2–4 and Figure 3. Version v3 compares 29 models. Overall accuracy (OA) is a weighted aggregate. GPT-4-0613 scores 4.01 versus 3.11 for Claude 3 Opus. In the database environment Claude Opus scores 51.7 versus 32.0 for GPT-4. Neither the median size nor OA alone explains the different failure classes. Averaged over models, invalid actions ended 64.1% of household runs and invalid formatting ended 53.3% of database runs. - Appendix J, Table 6 and Figures 6–10. The analysis distinguishes context limits, invalid formats, invalid actions and exhausted task budgets. Invalid format ended 10.4% of runs for open models versus 6.0% for commercial models. The corresponding rates for invalid actions were 13.6% and 4.6%. Interaction-length distributions, cases and model comparisons illustrate what the aggregates conceal.
