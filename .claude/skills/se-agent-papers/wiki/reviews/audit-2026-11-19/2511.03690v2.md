## Section review

- **1–3.** The paper traces the move from the shared V0 monolith to four V1 packages. The authors relate the redesign to application dependencies, configuration changes, and fragmented responsibility for conversation state. Figures 1–2 compare the architectures and illustrate SDK use. - **5, Tables 2–5 and Figure 8.** During a 15-day parallel deployment, system-attributed errors fell from 78 to 30 per 1,000 conversations. The analysis excluded LLM-provider errors. For 39,870 events across 433 conversations, median write time was 0.20 ms and median crash recovery time was 7.4 ms. The authors separately describe continuous testing and results for 14 models in five categories. On SWE-bench Verified, Sonnet 4 scored 68.0% in both V0 and V1. Sonnet 4.5 scored 64.6% and 72.8%, respectively. ## Independent verification

A drop of 48 errors per 1,000 conversations corresponds to **61.54% relative**. The audit identified two discrepancies. Page 13 claims crash recovery in under 20 ms even for the longest conversation, but Table 3 gives **18.9 ms for replay alone** and **32.1 ms for crash recovery** that includes an additional scan. The text also claims an advantage on three of five benchmarks. In Table 5, two values clearly exceed the published comparison results. For the third benchmark (SWE-bench Multimodal), footnote 4 indicates there was no published comparator.
