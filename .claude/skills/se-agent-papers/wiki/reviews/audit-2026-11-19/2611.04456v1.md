# How Agent Skills Evolve: A Large-Scale Empirical Study of Skills on GitHub

Audit dated 19 November 2026, covering all 12 pages of version `2611.04456v1`. [Publication card](../../sources/arxiv-2611.04456.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04456v1) · [Authors, license, and checksum (local metadata)](../../raw/metadata/2611.04456.metadata.json)

## Sections and evidence

- **I–II. Aim and background.** The authors study the history of files describing agent skills and their supporting resources. Figure 1 distinguishes metadata and procedures from scripts and supporting documents. The review places the study in the context of skill acquisition, retrieval, and updating. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=1) · [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=2) · [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=3)
- **III. Method.** The authors found 296,051 candidate files across 196,970 repositories. After filtering for content and revision history, they retained 84,200 skills from 7,776 repositories and 750,898 associated commits. Figure 2 shows the selection process. Table I and Figure 3 give taxonomies of components, motivations, and change types. Two people annotated examples and reconciled their labels. The model labeled the component affected by a change, allowing individual commits to have multiple labels. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=3) · [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=4) · [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=5) · [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=6)
- **IV, RQ1–2. Composition and frequency.** Figures 4–6 cover file composition and events after creation. Of the skills studied, 58.5% had fewer than five such events. RQ2–4 examine component changes in 5,628 particularly active skills. In this sample, 18.6% of changes concerned procedure execution and 4.7% concerned verification (Figure 7). [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=6) · [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=7) · [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=8)
- **IV, RQ3–4. Type and motivation.** The five most frequent change types in Table II account for 38.57% combined. Improving existing capabilities accounts for 50.37% of motivation labels (Figure 8). For commits labeled as security improvements, the authors counted high-risk signals raised by two scanners. Both scanned the same 876 commits. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=8) · [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=9)
- **V–VIII. Discussion, limitations, and materials.** Agreement with manual component labels differs between samples (0.881 and 0.913). Agreement was 0.816 for change type and 0.840 for motivation (Table IV). The discussion presents automated tests as future work. The data cover public repositories and, for RQ2–4, actively modified files. Pages 11–12 contain the availability statement and bibliography. ## Independent checks and decision

Both rows of Table III sum to 876. Averaging the rates 133/876 and 95/876 gives 13.01% across the two scanners. The overlap between them is unknown, so the rows cannot establish the number of distinct commits with improvements. A change in scanner warnings provides an indirect measure of risk in a running system. [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04456v1#page=9)

**Decision: retain `section_reviewed`.** The full PDF was read. Sections III and V refer to a supplement containing a change-mapping example, annotation prompts, and detailed agreement results. The authors’ address, `github.com/ZJU-CTAG/SkillEMP`, returned HTTP 404 in both the web interface and the public API at audit time. The arXiv v1 source archive contains the main text and figures. The supplement could not be found there. ## Follow-up search and agreement denominators, 20 November 2026

The co-author’s publication list links this study to its arXiv PDF. The public GitHub organization list did not include SkillEMP, and the repository address in the paper returned 404. The exact-title DataCite record has no related identifiers. No matching dataset turned up in the Zenodo title search or the Hugging Face dataset search for SkillEMP. The arXiv v1 source archive contains the paper sections and figures, including the taxonomy and the skill example, with no separate annotation appendix. [Co-author’s publications](https://zhonghaojiang.github.io/pub/) · [Authors’ GitHub organization](https://github.com/ZJU-CTAG) · [DataCite record](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.48550/arxiv.2611.04456) · [Source archive](https://arxiv.org/src/2611.04456v1)

Table IV gives sample sizes of 383 and 27 skills and 404 commits. Section V-A specifies that component labels are compared at the Markdown-block level and change and motivation labels are compared on aligned components, with accuracy averaged over commits. Consequently, multiplying 0.816 or 0.840 by 404 does not recover the number of correct labels. Verification requires the online appendix linked in the paper, particularly its annotation instructions, label definitions and detailed agreement results. It remains unclear whether authors have made the files available to users with special access. Review status remains `section_reviewed`.
