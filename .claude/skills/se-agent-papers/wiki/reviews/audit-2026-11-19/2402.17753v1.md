## Appendices and data verification

Appendix A, Figures 5–11, documents how the authors developed personas and graphs, summarized conversations, stored observations, selected images and manually corrected utterances. Appendix B gives the release conditions and sample counts. The five counts in Table 5 sum to 7,512 questions across 50 conversations. The review independently counted the objects in the file released by the authors — locomo10.json contains 10 conversations and 1,986 questions. It is a subset with a different sample size.
