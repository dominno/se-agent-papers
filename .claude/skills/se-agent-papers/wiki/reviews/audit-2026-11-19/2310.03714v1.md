## Review of sections and appendices

- Sections 1–3. A signature specifies each module’s inputs and outputs. The Python program composes prediction and retrieval steps. The examples show direct generation, the ChainOfThought module and the separate parameters that the compiler optimizes. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=5)
- Sections 4–5. The teleprompter runs the program or a teacher and filters execution traces using a metric. Accepted demonstrations are assigned to the corresponding modules, and validation can guide the choice of variant. The paper examines the program, model and compilation procedure as separate factors. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=7)
- Section 6, Table 1. The GSM8K experiment uses 200 training examples, 300 development examples and roughly 1,300 official test examples. GPT-3.5 with CoT+bootstrap+ensemble scores 88.3% on development and 81.6% on test, compared with 72.4% on test using manually written CoT demonstrations. Llama2-13B with reflection+ensemble scores 49.0 and 46.9 on development and test, respectively. The two columns distinguish model selection from test performance. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=9)
- Sections 7–8, Table 2. The HotPotQA fullwiki evaluation uses ColBERTv2 retrieval and a test sample of 1,000 questions. With GPT-3.5, multi-hop bootstrapping scores 39.6 on exact match and 43.8 on retrieval of the passage pair, compared with 31.2 and 40.8 under few-shot prompting. The asterisked ensemble score of 45.6 exact match is based on only half the test sample. [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=10) [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=11)
- Appendices A–C, Figures 1–8. Advanced signatures, comparisons with LangChain and LlamaIndex, and long prompt examples illustrate how a module interface separates the task from its instruction wording. These are construction examples.
