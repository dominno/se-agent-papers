# Long-form factuality in large language models

Independent audit on 19 November 2026 • 2403.18802v4 · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2403.18802.md)

Sections 1–3. LongFact provides separate questions about concepts and objects. SAFE decomposes responses into verifiable facts, disambiguates them, judges their relevance and searches for evidence. Section 4 and Figures 4–5. The manually segmented dataset contains 11,528 + 4,483 = 16,011 facts from 496 responses. Agreement was 72.0%. The authors reassessed 100 disagreements and sided with SAFE in 76 and the original human judgment in 19. They had access to a wider range of sources than the original annotators. This validation covers steps 2–4 after manual fact extraction. Sections 5–9, Tables 1–2. Recall@K measures the number of confirmed facts relative to the preferred count K. On 250 LongFact-Objects questions across 13 models, GPT-4-Turbo scores 95.0% F1@64 and 66.4% F1@178. Claude-3-Haiku has higher precision (92.8% vs. 91.7%) but lower F1@64 (73.5%). The score reflects answer length. Errors in retrieved sources and language limitations affect interpretation.
