# Plan-and-Solve Prompting: Improving Zero-Shot Chain-of-Thought Reasoning by Large Language Models

Review of version `2305.04091v3` • 19 November 2026 • 24 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04091v3) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2305.04091.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2305.04091.md)

## Sections and experimental evidence

- Sections 1–2. Figure 1 distinguishes arithmetic errors, omitted steps and misunderstandings of the task. PS requests a plan before the solution, while PS+ adds task-specific guidance. Figures 2–3 distinguish generating a solution from subsequently extracting the answer. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=4)
- Sections 3–4. Table 1 describes ten datasets. The main experiment uses text-davinci-003 with greedy decoding. Tables 2–4 compare accuracy on arithmetic, commonsense reasoning and symbolic tasks. The voting variant in Figure 4 draws ten samples at temperature 0.7. The text below the figure gives 58.7% on GSM8K without voting, while Table 2 gives 59.3% for PS+. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=6)
- Table 5 shows that performance depends on the prompt. On 100 GSM8K tasks in Table 6, CoT made 46 errors, PS 43, and PS+ 39. The 39 PS+ errors comprise five calculation mistakes, seven omissions, and 27 semantic errors. Figure 5 relates the presence of answer components to error categories. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.04091v3#page=8)
- Sections 5–8 cover prior work on reasoning and planning, conclusions, reliance on manually written prompts and ethical considerations. In Appendix A.1, Tables 7–16 give the prompts used for each dataset. In A.2, Tables 17–26 show sample responses from each dataset. For example, the last letters of the four names in Table 25 do spell “olah”. ## Independent verification

The six PS+ scores average (91.8 + 59.3 + 92.2 + 46.0 + 94.7 + 75.7)/6 = 76.62%. The displayed values round to 76.6%, while Table 2 reports 76.7%. Either value exceeds the CoT score of 70.4%. The earlier reference to eight demonstrations has been qualified: the few-shot comparison on AQuA used four.
