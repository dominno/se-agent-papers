# SWE-Bench Pro V2: A Cleaner, Harder-to-Game Coding Benchmark

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2610.24206v2`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2610.24206.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24206v2) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2610.24206.metadata.json)

## Findings and evidence

- **1–2. Rationale and prior research.** The unit of evaluation is a system with a particular model, reasoning level and runtime. Figure 1 illustrates why a subset with a wider spread of results can be more informative than the highest score on the public set. The authors also discuss other audits of alignment between instructions and tests. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=3)
- **3.1–3.3. Task audit and repair.** Figure 2 distinguishes the artifact audit, the failure audit and the engineers' decisions. Each task was assigned one primary reason for change in the categories of Table 1. Of 731 tasks, 391 were repaired, 89 removed and 251 affected only by changes common to the evaluation. A second engineer checked the revised instructions without seeing the reference solution. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=7)
- **3.4–3.5. Verification.** The authors changed the shared scoring system and isolated evaluation. Afterward, the reference solution passes all 642 tasks and the empty patch passes none. These checks establish behavior at those two reference points. Other verdicts require separate validation. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=8)
- **4.1–4.4. Main study.** Table 2 reports completion rates and Wilson intervals for 642 tasks alongside results for 51 Hard tasks. At least one system solved each task. Figure 3 distinguishes correcting the score for an unchanged patch from correcting the description in a way that changes the agent’s behavior. The paper also reports checks of run integrity. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=9) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=10)
- **4.5 and 5–6. Environment and benchmark saturation.** Table 3 and Figure 4 compare three models in three runtimes. For Inkling, the spread between the highest- and lowest-scoring runtimes was 7.5 percentage points. In other pairs, cost showed a larger difference. Limits include possible memorization of public patches during training and the scope of the independent evaluation. The references follow the ethics statement. - **Appendices A–C.** Of 54 Hard candidates, three were rejected. Instructions were changed after selection for 39 of the 51 retained tasks. Appendix B describes 5,778 trajectories, the retry rule and validation of the activity classifier. Table 4 gives discordant outcome pairs, intervals and exact tests. Appendix C documents the configuration, execution time and procedure for resolving disagreements in the main study. ## Independent check of the paper record

Opus 5 with Claude Code failed 4 of 642 tasks, giving 638/642 = 99.38%, rounded to 99.4%. Table 2 gives a 95% interval of 98.4–99.8%. The reported extremes on the Hard subset correspond to 27/51 and 50/51. For Inkling, the difference between Pi and OpenCode in Table 4 is (18 − 66)/642 = −7.48 percentage points. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=9) [PDF, p. 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.24206v2#page=16)

The description of the clean container on the card needed a narrower qualification. **Appendix B** states that in the runtime comparison in Section 4.5, each patch was evaluated once inside the agent’s container. The main study in Table 2 used separate evaluation in a clean container. The nine configurations in Table 3 therefore use a different protocol.
