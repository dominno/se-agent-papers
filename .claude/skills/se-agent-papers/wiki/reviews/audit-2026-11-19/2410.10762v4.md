## Independent verification

In Table 1, the best manually designed method averages 76.0 (CoT with voting). AFlow’s 80.3 gives a 4.3-point gain, or 4.3/76.0 ≈ 5.7% relative. Against ADAS at 67.2, the gain is 13.1 points, or 19.5% relative. Executing the learned workflow with DeepSeek costs $0.0291, about 4.57% of the $0.6371 for direct GPT-4o at comparable pass@1. The cost table covers execution on a held-out set.
