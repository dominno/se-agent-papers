# Precise Zero-Shot Dense Retrieval without Relevance Labels

Review of version `2212.10496v1` • 19 November 2026 • 11 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10496v1) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2212.10496.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2212.10496.md)

## Section review

- **1–2. Problem and related work.** The authors study retrieval without relevance judgments for query–document pairs or fine-tuning on the evaluated collections. The generator had previously been instruction-tuned. Figure 1 shows how the generated text mediates between the query and a real document. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=3)
- **3. The method.** Real and hypothetical documents use the same encoder, allowing candidates to be scored by dot product. Vectors from several samples can be averaged, optionally including the query vector itself. This approximation assumes a single dominant interpretation of the query. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=4)
- **4. Experiments, Tables 1–3.** The authors sampled text-davinci-003 at temperature 0.7 and used Pyserini for retrieval. On DL19 and DL20, HyDE improved on Contriever across all three metrics. On six BEIR tasks it improved nDCG@10 and Recall@100 over the same encoder. On TREC-COVID it remained below BM25 on both measures (59.3 versus 59.5 and 41.4 versus 49.8). MRR@100 on Mr.TyDi improved over mContriever in all four languages. For Bengali, BM25 scored 41.8 versus 41.3 for HyDE. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=6)
- **5. Analysis and Table 4.** FLAN, Cohere and GPT improved the base Contriever’s nDCG@10 on both TREC datasets. With the fine-tuned encoder, GPT scored 67.4 and 63.5 versus 62.1 and 63.2 for the encoder alone. Both weaker generators fell below that baseline in this setting. The models differ in both size and training. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.10496v1#page=6)
- **6, Bibliography and A.1.** The authors discuss bootstrapping a retriever and leave multi-step queries as an open question. Eight prompt variants follow the references, including generating a counterargument for ArguAna and responding in the chosen language for Mr.TyDi. Both years of web retrieval use the same prompt type. ## Independent verification

Counting the task columns gives 2 TREC datasets + 6 BEIR tasks + 4 Mr.TyDi languages = **12**. HyDE outperforms the corresponding base encoder in these 12 comparisons. The description on page 2 lists **11** datasets. The authors do not identify which datasets they combined, so the card reports the three components separately. Table 1 independently gives differences of 61.3 − 44.5 = 16.8 and 57.9 − 42.1 = 15.8 nDCG@10 points. The publisher’s record identifies the conference version.
