# Repoformer: Selective Retrieval for Repository-Level Code Completion

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 · version `2403.10059v2`. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2403.10059.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10059v2) · [Retrieval metadata](../../raw/metadata/2403.10059.metadata.json)

## Review of sections and appendices

- Sections 1–3 and Figures 1–2. Repoformer trains the generator to decide whether to include context from other files. Training labels come from comparing completions generated with and without context. A special token supports either a greedy decision or a probability threshold. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=4)
- §§4–5, Figure 3 and Tables 1–2. Models ranging from 1B to 16B parameters were compared on RepoEval and CrossCodeLongEval. On RepoEval's function-level task, 7B Repoformer passes 35.16% of tests with threshold-based retrieval and 30.77% with greedy selection, compared with 31.43% for StarCoderBase with retrieval always enabled. Additional context need not improve the result on an individual case. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=7)
- Tables 3–4 and Figures 4–5. The A100 timing assumes that the retrieval decision, generation without retrieval and the retrieval-enabled path all start in parallel. In Table 3, threshold-based selection for the 1B model yields a 27–28% speedup and improves edit similarity (ES). Greedy selection yields up to a 69% speedup at the cost of lower ES. Table 4 tests a small selector with a larger generator. The figures examine retrieval cost and budget. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10059v2#page=8)
- Sections 6–7, Table 5 and Figure 6. The controls distinguish training the retrieval decision from training the model to generate completions with retrieved material. The conclusion applies to the specified cost model and completion settings. An impact discussion and references follow. - Appendices A–D, Figures 7–10, Algorithms 1–2, and Table 6. The authors give indexing and query parameters, explain the use of right-hand context for infilling, and compare trial retrieval and trial generation. The algorithms construct datasets for snippets and complete functions. Table 6 gives dataset sizes and lengths. With dense retrieval in Table 8, greedy selection by the 1B model yields 71% speedup but reduces code similarity on both tasks. Section E.3 incorrectly attributes over 70% to the threshold variant, which achieves 33%. The final curves show how the threshold affects latency and quality.
