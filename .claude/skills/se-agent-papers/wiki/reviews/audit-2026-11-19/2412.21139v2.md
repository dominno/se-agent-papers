# Training Software Engineering Agents and Verifiers with SWE-Gym

Review of version `2412.21139v2` • 19 November 2026 • 21 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.21139v2) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2412.21139.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2412.21139.md)

## Sections, Tables and Appendices

- Sections 1–3. Table 1 compares SWE-Gym with other datasets offering executable tests. From 64,689 candidate tasks across 358 repositories, the authors built working environments for 2,438 tasks from 11 projects. The parenthetical counts in Table 2 come from the SWE-Bench test set. Figure 2 shows that Lite has a different project mix. For example, it contains five pandas tasks, compared with 737 in the full dataset. - §4. In OpenHands, training on successful teacher trajectories raised the 32B model's Verified score from 7.0% to 20.6%, even as the proportion of empty patches rose from 9.5% to 13.8% (Table 3). Further training on a mixture that included the model's own trajectories lowered its Lite score from 15.3% to 8.7%. Table 4 covers a separate experiment with MoatlessTools. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.21139v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.21139v2#page=6)
- Sections 5–6. Figures 1 and 3–5 compare the numbers of samples used in training and solution selection. With 16 OpenHands attempts, 42.8% of tasks received at least one correct solution. Selecting a solution with the verifier yielded 32.0%. Limitations include the work of configuring environments and modest self-improvement results. The paper closes with an impact discussion, acknowledgments and references. ## Independent verification

Table 10 gives 106 + 19 + 67 + 111 + 188 = 491 successful demonstrations. Table 7 lists 294 distinct tasks after deduplication. The paper record now distinguishes two results formerly attributed jointly to two models. For MoatlessTools with eight attempts on Lite, Figure 7 and Table 5 report 26.0%, while Section 5.1.2 reports 26.3%. For eight attempts on Verified, Figure 3 gives 29.3% and the prose gives 29.8%. Both give 32.0% with 16 attempts.
