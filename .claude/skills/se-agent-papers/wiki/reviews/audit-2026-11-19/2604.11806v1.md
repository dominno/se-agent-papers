## Sections 1–3. Method and scope of data

Meerkat searches a collection of trajectories for evidence that a property specified in natural language has been violated. Demonstrating a violation can require multiple traces. Algorithm 1 and Table 1 distinguish the labeled detection evaluation from the audit of four observational corpora without exhaustive annotations. The labeled evaluation reports precision–recall curves, average precision and AUROC. Additional human review reduces false positives in the open-ended audit. ## Sections 4–6 and Appendices A–J

With modified inputs, AP fell from 0.10 to 0.01 in one robustness experiment. The discussion and ethics statement describe the monitor’s scope and the risk of misattribution. Appendix B describes the domains, C formalizes violations on a finite sample, and D reports AP, AUROC, and results by workload, model, and domain. On CUA-SHADE-Arena the difference from a simpler monitor is small (AP 0.86 versus 0.84).
