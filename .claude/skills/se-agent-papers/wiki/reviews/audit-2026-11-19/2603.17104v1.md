# When the Specification Emerges: Benchmarking Faithfulness Loss in Long-Horizon Coding Agents

Review of version `2603.17104v1` • 19 November 2026 • 19 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17104v1) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2603.17104.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2603.17104.md)

## Section review

- **1–3, Tables 1–2 and Figure 1.** Two agent platforms are tasked with reproducing an ML project from its paper. The control receives the full specification upfront. The other condition receives it through successive synthetic issues. A fixed list of 371 components from 20 papers and a 0–4 scale are used to score the final repositories. Two independent annotators checked 120 component–run pairs. Their weighted κ values against the judge were 0.927 and 0.882. - **4. Results, Tables 3–4 and Figures 2–3.** IF50 weights semantic fidelity (MCF/4) and the proportion of exported symbols actually used (DIR) equally. Providing the whole specification upfront improved IF50 over incremental disclosure for 16/20 papers with Claude Code (mean difference 0.116, Wilcoxon p = 0.0003) and 14/20 with Codex (0.071, p = 0.012). On Codex, DIR accounts for most of the difference. MCF changes from 3.242 to 3.245. An implementation can pass its author’s tests while diverging from the previously agreed design. - **5–7.** ProjectGuard maintains an external record of design decisions and repository structure. The authors compare specification monitoring, memory continuity and test execution. On Claude Code, adding memory increases MCF from 2.718 to 3.000. On Codex, the gain is chiefly in integration, with improvement in 15 of the 19 cases showing a measurable change in DIR. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17104v1#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.17104v1#page=8)
- **Limitations and Appendix A.** The dataset contains ten ICML and ten NeurIPS papers from 2025 (Table 5). The appendix describes how requirements were obtained, how sequences were generated, and which papers were used. Conversations are synthetic. The use of two commercial platforms and the design of the judge limit extrapolation of the observed frequencies to other kinds of projects. Some per-paper ratios are undefined. For Claude Code, averaging the individual MCF recovery ratios in the table gives 51%. The corresponding mean for IF50 recovery is 80%. ## Independent verification

The aggregate MCF scores give (3.000 − 2.718)/(3.031 − 2.718) = **90,10%**. This result concerns recovery of the gap in **semantic fidelity** in Claude Code and is a ratio of differences between means. The primary metric is IF50, while the mean of the individual percentages in Table 6 is 51%. Table 3 also reproduces IF50 for Claude Code without memory: 2.718/8 + 0.149/2 = 0.41425.
