## Review of sections and appendices

- §§1–3 and Figure 1. The base model stays fixed. Successful runs yield templates with tests and usage histories. On subsequent tasks, the system can use a template directly, retrieve it as context or generate a solution without it. The authors distinguish accepting a record from tracking its usefulness later. - Section 4, Tables 1–4 and Figure 2. The five datasets use three different success metrics. On ALFWorld with GPT-4o-mini, the mean over three runs is 85.6% (standard deviation 3.8 percentage points), followed by AFlow at 59.2%. In the stepwise ablation, adding skill reuse raises the score from 38.8% to 80.6%. On MBPP, the method uses 2,230 tokens per task, compared with 787 for ExpeL. ## Independent verification

In Table 16, 67/134 cases run directly without further generation. The broader count of 101/134 includes 34 cases that later switch modes. The figure of 99/101 = 98.0% uses this broader denominator. Across six types, 111/134 = 82.84%, consistent with about 83% for a single run. The 85.6% figure covers three attempts.
