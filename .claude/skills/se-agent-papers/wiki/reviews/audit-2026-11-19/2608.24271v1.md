# Observability and Fault Injection for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems in Software Engineering

## Findings and evidence

- **I. Introduction.** Variation between runs and dependencies between agents make it difficult to identify the stage at which an error arose. The authors propose a common trace format for baseline and perturbed runs. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=1) [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=2)
- **II.A–C. Architecture.** Figure 1 shows inputs and artifacts. Figure 2 shows two stages and two model calls in the Planner→Coder demonstration. Table I distinguishes session, stage and communication attributes. Table II catalogs supported perturbations of message sending and receipt, tools and the model. Validation covers delays only. [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=4)
- **III. Validation.** On two systems the authors compare a baseline run with adding one second of delay at either of two locations. They report 30 ProgramDev tasks and five attempts per task under each condition. The coefficient is the increase in total time over the baseline divided by the injected delay. Table III reports means. The text also reports medians. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=4)
- **IV. Related work.** MAST and AgentFail classify failures, TRAIL and Who&When localize them, AEGIS and AgenTracer generate trajectories, and LumiMAS and AgentOps support observation. The comparison covers functionality. The study does not measure performance advantages over these systems. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=5)
- **V–VII. Restrictions, availability, termination.** Trace analysis is manual. Comparing pairs of runs automatically and ranking causes are planned work. Section VI points to a public repository for the demonstration and ChatDev integration. The references follow the acknowledgments. There is no further appendix. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=6) [Authors’ repository](https://github.com/vagabondboffin/llmmas-otel)

## Independent verification of the paper record

Table III gives means in model-delay/message-delay order of 1.053/1.295 for the demonstration and 48.1/59.2 for ChatDev. The text reports ChatDev medians of 13.9/6.6, reversing the ordering seen in the means. The data therefore do not establish that message delay had a greater effect in a typical ChatDev run. The factors describe additional time. The experiment does not isolate instrumentation overhead. The design implies 150 runs per condition and system (30 × 5). Table III reports no uncertainty intervals. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24271v1#page=4)
