## Appendices and independent check of the derivation

Appendices A–B describe the algorithm and baseline adaptations. Appendix C derives the reward from gradient alignment. Theorem C.1 treats the group’s mean reward as an independent baseline for each trajectory. As written, the same observation contributes to its own mean. For independent samples, reward R and zero-mean score S = ∇logπ, E[(Rᵢ − mean R)Sᵢ] = (1 − 1/K)E[RᵢSᵢ]. For K = 8, the factor is 7/8 before further normalization. The direction of the expectation can be preserved, but the formula does not establish the claimed unbiasedness. Theorem C.2 concerns scaling within a single group.
