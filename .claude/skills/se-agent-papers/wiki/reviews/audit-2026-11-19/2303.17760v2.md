## Sections 1–7

- §§1–3 and Figures 1–2. A human proposes an idea and a supporting agent refines it into a task. Models acting as user and assistant then exchange instructions and proposed solutions. Rules for starting and ending the conversation limit role reversals, repeated instructions and promises of work that is never carried out. ## Independent verification

In AI Society, 76.3% preferred the dialogue result, 10.4% preferred the single response and 13.3% were ties, totaling 100%. Appendix K.5 tells evaluators to select one solution, although the protocol and table allow ties. HumanEval is a separate experiment: after fine-tuning, the 7B model scored 14.0% pass@1 and 57.9% pass@100, versus 10.5% and 36.5% for LLaMA-7B. In Table 7, CAMEL* 33B averaged 64.0 versus 64.2 for LLaMA 65B, qualifying the authors’ claim of consistent improvement over 65B.
