## Section review

- Sections 1–3 and Figures 1–2. Information needs can change as a long answer is generated. FLARE-instruct lets the model request retrieval explicitly. FLARE-direct predicts the next sentence, checks its token probabilities and regenerates it after retrieval if needed. The query can be constructed by masking parts of the sentence or by generating a separate question. - §§4–5 and Figure 3. Comparisons cover no retrieval, single and repeated retrieval based on previously generated text, and question decomposition. 2WikiMultihopQA, StrategyQA, ASQA and WikiAsp differ in task, index and evaluation metrics. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06983v2#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06983v2#page=6) [PDF, p. 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06983v2#page=16)
- Section 6, Figures 4–5 and Tables 1–4. On 500 examples from 2Wiki, FLARE-direct scores 51.0 EM and 59.7 F1. Decomposition scores 47.8 EM and FLARE-instruct scores 42.4. Using the entire next sentence instead of the previous sentence raises EM from 39.0 to 48.8. A 32-token window scores 43.6, compared with 39.0 for the full preceding text. On StrategyQA, Table 2 gives FLARE 77.3 EM, compared with 72.9 without retrieval. - Tables 5–6 and Sections 7–9. Masking at β = 0.4 raises exact match from 0.488 to 0.510. Both query constructions score 53.4 on UniEval for WikiAsp. The paper shows no significant gain on Wizard of Wikipedia or ELI5. The evaluated tasks assume access to external evidence. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06983v2#page=8) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.06983v2#page=9)
- Appendices A–D. Implementation and dataset details and the hyperparameters in Tables 7–9 specify BM25 retrieval over Wikipedia for three tasks and Bing with four domains excluded for WikiAsp. Sections D.1–D.10 document retrieval prompts, queries and demonstrations for deduction, answering ambiguous questions with and without hints, and aspect-based summarization.
