# WebArena: A Realistic Web Environment for Building Autonomous Agents

Audit opened on 19 November 2026 for version `2307.13854v4`, 22 pages. [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2307.13854.md) · [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854v4) · [Authors, license and checksum](../../raw/metadata/2307.13854.metadata.json)

## Sections, Tables and Appendices

- Sections 1–2. The authors provide four types of working websites, supporting tools and knowledge sources. The environment state can be restored. The agent observes each page and acts through the browser. The simulated roles differ in permissions and account histories. - Section 3 and Table 1. The authors constructed 812 instructions from 241 templates covering information retrieval, navigation and content modification. Some tasks are infeasible. The evaluator compares the answer or resulting state with a reference, so multiple action sequences can succeed. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=7)
- Sections 4–7, Tables 2–4. GPT-4 achieved 14.41% overall without a prompt to give up, compared with 11.70% when prompted. The prompt helped on infeasible tasks and hurt on feasible ones. Table 3 shows variation within templates, and Table 4 compares the scope of other benchmarks. The authors describe failures including premature stopping and incorrect webpage actions. - A.1–A.5. Details of the implementation, resets, users and distribution of instructions define the scope of the four domains. Human performance depends on domain knowledge and was measured on a separate sample. [PDF, p. 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=15) [PDF, p. 16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=16)
- Appendices A.6–A.9 and Tables 5–6. A limit of 30 transitions and explicit stopping rules define the budget. Manual review agreed with fuzzy answer matching on 39 of 40 examples. An additional test of date and time formats, with 900 examples each, assesses a narrower capability. The final pages provide instructions and examples for agents with and without reasoning. ## Independent check of the paper record

14.41% corresponds to **117 of the 812 agent tasks**and 78.24% is **133 of 170** tasks evaluated by five computer science graduate students. The study does not provide paired human and agent scores for all 812 cases. In Table 2, removing the hint raises GPT-4’s overall score by 2.71 percentage points, although correct identification of infeasible tasks falls from 77.78% to 44.44%.
