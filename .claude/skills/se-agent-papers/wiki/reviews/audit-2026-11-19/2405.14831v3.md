# HippoRAG: Neurobiologically Inspired Long-Term Memory for Large Language Models

Review of version `2405.14831v3` • 19 November 2026 • 31 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831v3) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2405.14831.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2405.14831.md)

## Review of chapters and tables

- §§1–3. Drawing an analogy with human memory, the authors separate index construction from query processing. An LLM extracts entities and relation triples. At query time, Personalized PageRank propagates probability mass through the graph from entities in the question, and the system then ranks passages. Table 1 describes the corpora. Figures 1–2 show the system design. - Sections 4–5, Tables 2–7. Retrieval recall (R@2/R@5), answer correctness (EM/F1) and retrieval of all required sources measure different outcomes. Adding the graph to single-step ColBERTv2 raises mean R@5 from 65.6 to 72.9 but lowers HotpotQA R@5 from 79.3 to 77.7. IRCoT with HippoRAG/ColBERTv2 averages 78.2. Ablations examine the extractor, synonyms and propagation of probability mass. ## Independent verification

Table 17 gives online retrieval costs of $0.10 per 1,000 queries for HippoRAG and $1–3 for IRCoT. The cost ratio is 10–30, correcting the earlier card’s 10–20. Retrieval times of 3 minutes and 20–40 minutes correspond to a speedup of about 6.7–13.3×. At 10,000 passages, graph preparation adds a separate cost. In Table 4, single-step HippoRAG scores 55.0 F1 on HotpotQA, compared with 58.4 for IRCoT/ColBERTv2.
