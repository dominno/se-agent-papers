# LightRAG: Simple and Fast Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Independent audit on 19 November 2026 • 2410.05779v3 · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2410.05779.md)

## Section-by-section review

- **1–3, Figure 1.** An LLM extracts entities and relations from document passages to build the index. The system removes duplicates and retains links to the text. Specific keywords retrieve entities, while broader keywords retrieve relations. The generator’s context also includes graph neighbors. Adding documents merges their graphs with existing nodes and edges. - **4.1–4.2, Table 1.** For each of four UltraDomain corpora, the model generated five roles, five goals per role, and five questions per goal. GPT-4o-mini was used both within LightRAG and to judge answer pairs, with presentation order reversed. Each comparison covered comprehensiveness, diversity, support for understanding, and overall preference. The overall preference rate for LightRAG against NaiveRAG ranged from 60.0% to 84.8%. The difference relative to GraphRAG was smaller. - **4.3, Table 2.** Each variant removing local retrieval, global retrieval, or source passages was compared with NaiveRAG. On Mix, the judge preferred full LightRAG overall in 60.0% of cases and the variant without local retrieval in 64.8%. Removing the original passages raised the Agriculture overall win rate from 67.6% to 74.4%. On Mix, however, the overall win rate fell to 55.6% even though diversity improved. - **4.4, Table 3.** The question row asks about methods for normalizing feature values. Both answers and the judge’s explanation address evaluation metrics for movie recommendations. The judge chose LightRAG. In v3 this example cannot establish relevance to the printed question. The appendix gives a movie-recommendation question that does match the answers. - **4.5, Figure 2.** For Legal, 610 communities at 1,000 tokens per community yield 610,000 tokens for the GraphRAG configuration studied. The authors report fewer than 100 tokens for LightRAG and one call for keyword generation and retrieval. These figures cover retrieval only. The cost estimate for updating a larger corpus assumes that GraphRAG rebuilds its community reports. Table 1 does not measure retrieved-document relevance against gold labels. ## Independent verification

The design gives 5 × 5 × 5 = 125 questions per corpus and 500 in total. In the comparison with GraphRAG, the Overall row gives LightRAG preference rates of 54.8%, 52.0%, 52.8% and 49.6%. On Mix, the judge preferred LightRAG for diversity in 64.0% of comparisons, while its overall preference rate was below 50%.
