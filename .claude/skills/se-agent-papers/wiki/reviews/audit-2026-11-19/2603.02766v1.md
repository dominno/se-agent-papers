## Sections and supplementary material

- Sections 1–2, Figure 1, and Algorithm 1. After a task failure, a model proposes a change to the skill instructions. Another agent writes the change, and a validation score determines whether the variant joins the bounded pool of best programs. Training, validation, and test tasks serve distinct roles. At 10% tolerance, Table 1 reports 82.4% for the combined skills, slightly below the best individual variant at 82.5%. The authors give one run per configuration and leave variation across seeds for future study. One learned instruction transferred unchanged to a stratified sample of 128 BrowseComp examples and yielded the reported change from 43.5% to 48.8%. The review distinguishes transferring a skill from updating model weights. The evidence for transfer covers this particular task pair. ## Independent check

At zero tolerance, Table 1 gives **68.1 − 60.6 = 7.5 percentage points**, whereas the text and caption for Figure 2 **67.9 − 60.6 = 7.3 percentage points** The arXiv source table confirms that the discrepancy is present in the authors’ material. Separately, the SealQA difference is 38.7 − 26.6 = 12.1 percentage points. The prose and table values are recorded separately, since the difference cannot be attributed to an individual run.
