## Review of sections and appendices

- §§1–2. Table 1 and Figures 1–2 distinguish a single-call test from a conversation in which the application's state changes. The agent, user simulator and environment each see a different view of the history. Independent steps may occur in different orders. Meeting a prohibited condition sets the score for the entire trajectory to zero (Figure 3, Equation 1). With GPT-4o in both roles, adding a description of the user's knowledge and examples reduced simulator hallucinations from 12.4% to 6.97% and failures to follow instructions from 6.20% to 0.77% (Table 2). ## Independent verification

Table 6 counts 152 single-call and 656 multiple-call cases, giving 808 in all. Adding the 224 cases with insufficient information reproduces the 1,032 cases in Table 4. In Table 9, replacing the default prompt with ReAct changes scores by −0.1 to +0.9 points across four models and leaves their ranking unchanged. Comparing Tables 5 and 11, GPT-4o has the highest mean score and uses 12.2 turns on average, versus 11.6 for Claude-3-Opus, whose mean score is 69.2.
