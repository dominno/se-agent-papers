# Evo-Harness: Context-to-Harness Skill Compilation for Self-Evolving Agents

Review of version `2608.15071v2` • 19 November 2026 • 16 pages. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15071v2) · [Metadata and license](../../raw/metadata/2608.15071.metadata.json) · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2608.15071.md)

## Section review

- **1–3. From execution to guidance.** With fixed model weights, the model receives selected entries relevant to its task. After execution it proposes tips based on the trace and feedback. A separate role compares proposals with existing entries and updates them after a batch of tasks. Figures 1–3 and Algorithm 1 distinguish general guidance from task-specific procedures. - **4.1–4.3, Tables 1–2.** With Opus 4.6, scores for the full variant on CL-Bench, TerminalBench-2, SWE-bench Lite, τ-bench and WebArena-Infinity were 34.02%, 73.03%, 67.00%, 76.97% and 76.25%, respectively. Each exceeds both No Evolve and XSkill. On CL-Bench the aggregate gain does not extend to every category. For empirical discovery and simulation, Opus 4.6 drops from 20.6% to 18.1%. - **4.4–4.6. Ablations and transfer.** Figure 4 groups the content of guidance. In Table 3, topic-specific entries approach the full result on CL-Bench (33.70% versus 34.02%), while general entries approach it on SWE-bench Lite (66.67% versus 67.00%). In Figure 5, transfer from a separate dataset reaches 73.4%, versus 68.8% without updates and 75.0% with test-time updates. In Figure 6 both update settings for Sonnet fall below baseline. In Table 4 model self-assessment worsens both results. On SWE-bench Lite, minimal external feedback gives 67.33%, slightly above the 67.00% with full diagnostics. - **5, limitations and bibliography.** The evaluation covers a single executor, natural-language guidance and software environments. Trace comparisons used to explain the gains may conflate the effects of several tips. The authors report averaging the main results over three runs and using seed 42 to shuffle tasks. ## Independent verification

On TerminalBench-2, the difference from No Evolve is 73.03 − 62.92 = **10.11 percentage points**, and the difference from XSkill is 73.03 − 66.29 = **6.74 percentage points** Table 5 lists 89 tasks. Table 2 and Figure 6 show conditions with no improvement, limiting generalization of the main result across categories and models.
