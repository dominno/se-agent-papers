# Where LLM Agents Fail and How They can Learn From Failures

Independent audit on 19 November 2026 • 2509.25370v1 · [Paper record](../../sources/arxiv-2509.25370.md)

§§1–2. From more than 500 unsuccessful trajectories, the authors selected 200 for annotation: 100 from ALFWorld, 50 from WebShop and 50 from GAIA. Ten annotators identified the step where each error originated, the module involved and the error type. Interrater agreement was κ = 0.55. The study distinguishes environment errors from errors in agent memory, reflection, planning or action. Sections 3–4 and Table 1. AgentDebug localizes the first divergence and retries from that step up to five times. Its All Correct rates are 21%, 14% and 38% across three domains. Their unweighted mean is 24.33%. Weighting by sample counts of 100, 50 and 50 would give 23.5%. Under the table’s convention the gain over the best baseline (0.3%) is 24.0 percentage points. Identifying the step alone succeeds in 45% versus 28% of cases. §§4–7 and Figures 5–8. On ALFWorld, successes rise from 48 on the first attempt to 74 with Qwen3-8B, and from 21 to 55 with GPT-4o-mini. Interpretation of the reported gains depends on the choice of comparison strategies and the limit on attempts. The propagation analysis examines how errors can spread from memory into a plan.
