## Sections and Tables

- Sections 1–3 and Figures 1–2. Selection acts on task instructions together with instructions for mutating them. Operators can modify the mutation instructions as well. Nine operators fall into five classes. Candidates are scored on a batch of 100 training examples. ## Independent verification

Comparing the “Few-shot APE” and “Few-shot PE” columns in Table 9 gives PB 10 wins, 11 ties and 3 losses. The “21 of 24” in the text counts ties as well as wins. The caption claims PB outperformed APE on 21 tasks, which the table does not support. In Table 1, zero-shot and few-shot PB scored 83.9% and 83.5% on GSM8K. Adding examples did not improve that result.
