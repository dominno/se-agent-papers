## Sections 1–3. Separation of task and execution mechanism

TraceTrial-Bench defines separate criteria for the agent’s completion of its task and correct operation of its harness. Tables 1–5 describe the task contract, event types, evidence sources, verdict rules and attribution labels. A tool’s record can differ from the information that reached the model. Coincident errors identify a candidate for further investigation. ## Sections 4–6. Population, metrics and independent recalculation

Four environments × nine model configurations × 95 tasks yield 3,420 combinations, with one run selected per combination. Tables 6–7 distinguish passing all criteria, the mean partial score across runs and micro-averaging across criteria. Tables 8–9 break down mechanisms and task groups. One task represents the verification category. Table 10 reproduces the main result. Of 1,351 runs satisfying all task criteria, 286 also satisfied all harness criteria. The remaining 1,065 are 31.14% of all 3,420 runs and 78.83% of the 1,351 task successes. The cells sum to 286 + 1,065 + 369 + 1,700 = 3,420. Among 1,700 runs failing both layers, the authors identified a potential link in 842 (49.5%). Causal interpretation requires intervention or stronger evidence. ## Independent source check

The evidence scheme distinguishes what the model saw from what was observed during execution. The protocol selects one run for each of 4 × 9 × 95 combinations. Table 10 yields 1,065/3,420 = 31.14% that passed the task despite environment deficiencies and 286/3,420 = 8.36% that passed both layers. Table 9 assigns one task to Verification. Of 1,700 runs failing both layers, 842 had an identified link between failures. Co-occurrence alone leaves causality unresolved.
