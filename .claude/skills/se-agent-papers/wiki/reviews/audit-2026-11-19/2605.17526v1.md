## Sections and Appendices

- Sections 1–3 and Table 1. The enterprise SaaS specifications contain 30 project-based tasks across six areas. A dependency graph scores verified properties and propagates failed prerequisites to descendant nodes. The authors validated the requirements and checked that the test suites work. ## Independent check

The rows of Table 13 sum to 289 + 854 + 1216 + 1179 + 1023 + 809 = **5,370 nodes**. At a different level of aggregation, the failure breakdown gives 63.5% unstable deployments + 32.1% superficially available units = **95.6% of the units analysed**.
