Sections 1–2, Figure 1 and Tables 1–2. The factual benchmark divides questions into eight types. Three tasks use the same questions, providing up to five pages in the first, an additional simulated graph in the second, and up to 50 pages plus the graph in the third. Section 4 distinguishes human scoring, which awards a half point for an acceptable answer, from automatic scoring, which awards a full point. Both award zero for no answer and −1 for an incorrect answer. In Section 5, Table 5 and Figure 3, moving GPT-4 Turbo from no RAG to Task 3 raises accuracy by 10.1 percentage points but incorrect answers by 16.6 points. Truthfulness falls from 20.0 to 13.4. The narrative generalizes an advantage for Task 3 over Task 2, although this model falls from 16.2 to 13.4.
