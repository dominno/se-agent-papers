# Self-Evolving Coding Agents

## Research findings

**Method.** The survey covers 108 methods that retain and reuse coding experience, distinguishing updates to resources, architecture and model parameters. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=7) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=9) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=10) [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=11)

**Key result.** The synthesis matches each of 27 representative methods to its mechanism of change and a result from its original benchmark. The table mixes improvements with final scores, which precludes a common performance ranking. [PDF, p. 32](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=32)

**Limitations and scope.** A difficult task or a passing test after a fix does not establish transfer to future tasks. Evaluation requires separating adaptation from measurement and accounting for retained state and cost. [PDF, p. 25](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=25) [PDF, p. 35](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=35) [PDF, p. 36](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.03392v4#page=36)
