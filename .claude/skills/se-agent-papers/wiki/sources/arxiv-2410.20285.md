# SWE-Search: Enhancing Software Agents with Monte Carlo Tree Search and Iterative Refinement

**Paper:** [2410.20285](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20285) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20285) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20285)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** SWE-Search combines Monte Carlo tree search with qualitative feedback for the code-repair agent. [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20285v6#page=10)

**Key result.** Across five SWE-Search configurations, the share of tasks resolved was 4.96 percentage points higher on average than with Moatless-Adapted. The paper reports 23% by dividing each gain by the final score. Relative to the corresponding baselines, the mean gain is approximately 29.5%. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20285v6#page=8)

**Limitations and scope.** Deeper search requires additional inference-time compute. The comparison therefore depends on the available budget. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.20285v6#page=1)
