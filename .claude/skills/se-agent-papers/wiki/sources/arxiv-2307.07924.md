# ChatDev: Communicative Agents for Software Development

**Paper:** [2307.07924](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.07924) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.07924)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** ChatDev connects design, coding and testing through a chain of conversations between pairs of agents. Solutions pass between phases, and within a phase an assistant can ask the other role for clarification. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=4) [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=5)

**Key result.** On SRDD, 88.00% of ChatDev applications were executable, compared with 41.45% for MetaGPT and 35.83% for GPT-Engineer. Their composite Quality scores were 0.3953, 0.1523 and 0.1419, respectively. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=6)

**Limitations and scope.** A program that runs and contains no placeholders may still fail to meet requirements. The authors describe simplified prototypes. In the same settings, the single-agent GPT-Engineer required substantially less time and fewer tokens. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=6) [PDF, p. 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.07924v5#page=9)
