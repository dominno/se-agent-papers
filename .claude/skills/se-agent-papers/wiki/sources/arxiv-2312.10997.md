# Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey

**Paper:** [2312.10997](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10997) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.10997)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** The review organizes RAG publications by retrieval, generation and how retrieved material is incorporated into the answer. Table I records the data source, granularity and stage of use. Tables II–IV map tasks, metrics and evaluation frameworks. [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=6) [PDF, p. 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=12) [PDF, p. 13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=13) [PDF, p. 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=14) [PDF, p. 15](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=15)

**Key result.** The survey distinguishes basic, advanced and modular RAG as well as one-time, iterative, recursive and adaptive retrieval. These are categories of methods and do not represent the results of a single performance comparison. [PDF, p. 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=2) [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=3) [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=4) [PDF, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=10) [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=11) [PDF, p. 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=12)

**Limitations and scope.** Even a relevant passage can lead to an unfaithful answer. The survey distinguishes retrieval quality from generation quality and notes the absence of a mature, standardized evaluation of all aspects of RAG. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=3) [PDF, p. 12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=12) [PDF, p. 14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.10997v5#page=14)
