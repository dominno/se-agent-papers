# AFlow: Automating Agentic Workflow Generation

**Paper:** [2410.10762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10762) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10762)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** AFlow searches among agent workflows expressed as code, selecting, modifying and running candidate workflows on validation data. The optimizer and executor can use different models. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762v4#page=5) [PDF, p. 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762v4#page=6) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762v4#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762v4#page=8)

**Key result.** Across six datasets with GPT-4o-mini, the mean score was 80.3, compared with 76.0 for the best manual approach tested and 67.2 for ADAS. The first margin was 4.3 score points, or 5.7% relative. [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10762v4#page=8)

**Limitations and scope.** The study averaged three kinds of metrics across different datasets. The HumanEval cost comparison covers execution of the discovered procedures. Optimizing them required additional rounds. The two open-ended demonstrations each use a single prompt.
