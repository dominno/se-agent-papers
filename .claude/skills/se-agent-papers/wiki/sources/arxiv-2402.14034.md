# AgentScope: A Flexible yet Robust Multi-Agent Platform

**Paper:** [2402.14034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14034) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14034)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** AgentScope uses actors and messages. Developers can define control flow procedurally and connect local and remote agents. [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=4) [PDF, p. 19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=19) [PDF, p. 20](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=20)

**Key result.** The authors document eight applications, including distributed dialogue, RAG and SQL. The appendices contain a conversation transcript and a Werewolf game from role assignment through the final vote. [PDF, p. 21](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=21) [PDF, p. 26](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=26) [PDF, p. 28](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=28) [PDF, p. 34](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=34) [PDF, p. 37](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=37)

**Limitations and scope.** The paper documents platform capabilities. Its example runs do not provide a comparative measure of answer quality or the probability of recovering from an error. [PDF, p. 13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=13) [PDF, p. 30](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=30) [PDF, p. 34](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=34)

## Overview

The paper presents a multi-agent platform with messaging, monitoring, fault tolerance and support for distributed execution. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.14034v2#page=1)
