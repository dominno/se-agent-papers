# WebArena: A Realistic Web Environment for Building Autonomous Agents

**Paper:** [2307.13854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13854) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.13854)  
**Review status:** `fulltext_reviewed` · **Full text reviewed:** **yes**

## Research findings

**Method.** WebArena evaluates the functional outcome of multi-step work on live sites for commerce, forums, software development and content management. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=1)

**Key result.** GPT-4 with reasoning and no hint about infeasibility scored 14.41% on 812 tasks. The human result of 78.24% came from a separate sample of 170 tasks, one per selected template. [PDF, p. 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=5) [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=7) [PDF, p. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=8)

**Limitations and scope.** The evaluation covers four selected domains. Generalization to other websites needs further evidence. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=1)

## Overview

The benchmark provides reproducible websites and realistic multi-step tasks for browser-based agents. [PDF, p. 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.13854v4#page=1)
