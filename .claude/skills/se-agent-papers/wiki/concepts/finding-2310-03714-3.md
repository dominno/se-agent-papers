# The meaning of a DSPy score depends on what the metric measures

**Reviewer’s interpretation and caveats:** On HotpotQA, the authors report exact match for the answer and retrieval of the correct pair of passages. A higher exact-match score alone does not establish better retrieval. [PDF, p. 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=7) · [PDF, p. 11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03714v1#page=11)
