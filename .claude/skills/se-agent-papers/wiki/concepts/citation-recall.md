# Citation completeness

The proportion of claims requiring citations that have supporting citations.

**Status:** This page groups papers by topic. An entry in the list indicates **a topical relationship**. It does not imply that every claim is confirmed or that one paper cites another.

## Papers on this topic (1)

- [Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations](../sources/arxiv-2305.14627.md) — ALCE evaluates the correctness, quality and completeness of citations in model-generated answers.
