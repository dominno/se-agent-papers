# ALCE measures answer correctness, citation recall and citation precision separately

**Finding from the paper:** Citation recall measures whether the cited passages jointly support the sentences in an answer. Precision measures the contribution of each citation. The authors assess factual correctness separately. [PDF, p. 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=3) · [PDF, p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14627v2#page=4)

**Source:** [Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations](../sources/arxiv-2305.14627.md) — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627)
