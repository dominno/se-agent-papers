# More available memory does not guarantee successful multistep retrieval

**Reviewer’s interpretation and caveats:** On nested-key retrieval, success depends on both model and depth. Baseline GPT-3.5 dropped to 0% at one level of nesting. MemGPT with GPT-3.5 or GPT-4 Turbo lost accuracy from two levels onward, while MemGPT with GPT-4 maintained its score.
